Common questions

Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. There is no account.

How do I move my progress to another device? More → Export save, copy the text, then More → Import save on the other device.

Are ads required? No. Ads only play when you tap a ▶ button for a bonus.

How do I start over? More → Reset progress (tap twice).
