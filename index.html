Cosmic Chomp — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 (analytics and crash reporting added)

Cosmic Chomp (“the app”) is made by Sungheon Jeong. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What we collect

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

Gameplay statistics (Google Firebase Analytics). To understand how the game is played and to balance it, the app sends anonymous events such as finishing the tutorial, reaching a new stage, defeating a boss, a Big Bang, claiming a daily reward or an offline reward, watching an optional reward video, and changing a setting. Firebase links these to a random app-instance identifier, not to your name, email or contacts. The app does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes or hits an error, a report with technical details (device model, OS version, the error and where in the code it happened) is sent so we can fix it.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Ads only play when you choose to watch one. Google may collect and use device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interaction data and diagnostics to serve, limit and measure ads.

The app does not use App Tracking Transparency and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your choices

In the app, open More → Privacy. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), you can change your ad consent there under “Ad privacy choices”. To report an ad, tap the AdChoices / ⓘ icon on the ad.

Deleting your data

Use More → Reset progress, or delete the app. We hold no copy of your game progress. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google Firebase for its standard retention period (Analytics: 14 months; Crashlytics: 90 days). To ask for deletion of this data, email us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
